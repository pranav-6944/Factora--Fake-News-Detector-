What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Example Cookie

{
  "name": "session_id",
  "value": "abc123xyz",
  "expires": "2025-12-31",
  "domain": "factora.com"
}

How Cookies Work

  • When you visit FacTora, our server sends a cookie to your browser
  • Your browser stores the cookie on your device
  • When you return, your browser sends the cookie back to our server
  • This helps us recognize you and remember your preferences
Cookies Are Safe:

Cookies cannot access or read data from your device, carry viruses, or install malware. They only store small amounts of text information specific to the website that created them.

Cookie Duration

Cookies have different lifespans:

Session Cookies

Until browser closes

These temporary cookies are deleted when you close your browser. They help maintain your session while you navigate our site.

Short-Term Cookies

1-30 days

These cookies remain on your device for a short period to remember recent preferences and activities.

Long-Term Cookies

Up to 2 years

These persistent cookies help us recognize you on return visits and maintain your long-term preferences.

Cookie Expiration Timeline

Session Login cookies, CSRF tokens
24 Hours - 30 Days Analytics, performance tracking
6 Months - 1 Year Preferences, settings, theme
Up to 2 Years Long-term analytics, user recognition

Browser Cookie Controls

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

Google Chrome

  1. Click the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy and security
  3. Click Cookies and other site data
  4. Choose your preferred cookie settings
Learn More

Mozilla Firefox

  1. Click the three lines (☰) in the top-right corner
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security
  3. Scroll to Cookies and Site Data
  4. Choose your preferred settings
Learn More

Safari

  1. Open Safari and click Safari in the menu bar
  2. Go to Preferences → Privacy
  3. Manage Cookies and website data
  4. Choose your blocking options
Learn More

Microsoft Edge

  1. Click the three dots (...) in the top-right corner
  2. Go to Settings → Cookies and site permissions
  3. Click Manage and delete cookies
  4. Configure your cookie settings
Learn More
Mobile Browsers:

For mobile devices, cookie settings are typically found in your browser's settings menu under Privacy or Security. The exact location varies by browser and operating system.

08

Impact of Disabling Cookies

While you can disable cookies, doing so may affect your experience on FacTora:

If You Disable Essential Cookies

  • You cannot log in to your account
  • Security features will not work
  • Form submissions may fail
  • Core functionality will be unavailable

If You Disable Functional Cookies

  • Preferences won't be saved
  • You'll need to set preferences each visit
  • Personalization features won't work
  • User experience may be degraded

If You Disable Analytics Cookies

  • Core features continue to work
  • We won't track your usage patterns
  • Harder for us to improve the service
  • No significant impact on functionality
Recommended Settings:

For the best experience, we recommend accepting all cookies. However, you can safely disable Analytics and Performance cookies if you prefer, and still use FacTora effectively.
