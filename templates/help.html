Getting Started with FacTora

What is FacTora?

FacTora is an AI-powered fake news detection platform that helps you verify the authenticity of news headlines and articles. Our advanced machine learning algorithms analyze text patterns, language use, and credibility indicators to determine whether content is likely to be real or fake.

How to Create an Account

  1. Click the "Register" button in the top navigation
  2. Enter your desired username and password
  3. Confirm your password
  4. Click "Create Account" to complete registration
  5. You'll be automatically logged in and can start using FacTora

Making Your First Prediction

  1. Navigate to the Dashboard after logging in
  2. Paste or type a news headline in the text box
  3. Click "Analyze" to start the prediction
  4. Wait a few seconds for the AI to process
  5. View your result showing REAL or FAKE with confidence percentage

How to Use FacTora

Your dashboard provides an overview of your activity:

  • Total Predictions: Number of analyses you've performed
  • Accuracy Stats: Your feedback accuracy percentage
  • Recent Feedback: Latest feedback you've submitted
  • Quick Predict: Input box for instant analysis

The History page shows all your past predictions:

  • Search: Find specific predictions by keyword
  • Filter: View only REAL or FAKE predictions
  • Sort: Order by date or confidence level
  • Export: Download your data as CSV
  • Delete: Remove individual predictions

Your feedback helps improve our AI:

  • After each prediction, you can mark it as "Accurate" or "Wrong"
  • This feedback trains our model to be more accurate
  • Your feedback history is visible in your dashboard
  • You can change your feedback at any time

Check real-time news headlines:

  • Browse current news from trusted sources
  • Click "Analyze" on any headline to check it
  • View source credibility information
  • Click "Read More" to visit the original article

Download your prediction history:

  • Go to History page
  • Click "Export CSV" button
  • Your data downloads as a spreadsheet
  • Includes headline, prediction, confidence, and timestamp

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI?

Our AI model achieves 95%+ accuracy on test data. However, no system is perfect. We recommend using FacTora as one of several tools for fact-checking.

How long does analysis take?

Most predictions complete in 2-5 seconds. Complex headlines may take slightly longer as the AI performs deeper analysis.

What languages are supported?

Currently, FacTora primarily supports English text. We're working on adding support for more languages in future updates.

Is my data secure?

Yes! We use industry-standard encryption and never share your data with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Is FacTora free to use?

Yes, FacTora is completely free for all users. We believe fact-checking should be accessible to everyone.

Is there a mobile app?

Currently, FacTora is web-based and works on mobile browsers. A dedicated mobile app is planned for future release.

What if I find an error?

Please use the feedback buttons to report incorrect predictions. This helps us improve the AI. For technical issues, contact support.

Can I use this for research?

Yes! FacTora can be used for academic research. Contact us for API access and bulk analysis options.

Troubleshooting

Prediction Not Working

  • Ensure you're logged in
  • Check your internet connection
  • Try refreshing the page
  • Clear browser cache and cookies
  • Try a different browser

Can't Log In

  • Double-check your username and password
  • Ensure Caps Lock is off
  • Clear browser cookies
  • Try incognito/private browsing mode
  • Contact support if issue persists

History Not Loading

  • Refresh the page
  • Check if you have any predictions
  • Try clearing search/filter options
  • Disable browser extensions
  • Try on a different device

Mobile Issues

  • Use latest version of your browser
  • Enable JavaScript
  • Clear mobile browser cache
  • Try desktop mode if needed
  • Ensure stable internet connection
